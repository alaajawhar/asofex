## Rationale per screenshot
1. Names the habit (Share) and the promise; matches the subtitle "Save ideas from any app". Mascot catches a card flying out of the share sheet.
2. The wall is the product's signature visual and answers "nothing was in one place".
3. Owner priority: MCP lets any AI app read your saved ideas. Unique in the category. Marked Pro. Gemini left out (consumer app has no MCP). Logos shown by owner decision; if App Review rejects, swap to text-only names.
4. Answers "it added it as a link" (mymind review): Ideafex keeps title, author, preview, text.
5. Category's unmet promise: getting it back. Free search covers text, titles, captions, so no transcript claim here.
6. Answers "folders are work": drag, nest, multi-folder.
7. Differentiator vs Notion/Raindrop reviews ("needs an offline mode desperately").
8. Identity callout requested by the owner (2026-10-16): creators, writers, students, ADHDers.
9. Close: restates "inbox", price truth (free + Pro), shows light mode.
