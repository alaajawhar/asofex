# Ideafex: Idea Inbox positioning  (updated 2026-10-16)

## Statement
For people who save videos, posts and articles from every app and then never find them again, Ideafex is the idea inbox that keeps everything on one searchable wall with its source, even offline, unlike bookmarks, screenshots and notes apps, which scatter saves and keep only a bare link.

## The one promise (screenshot 1)
"Save ideas from any app"  Why it stops the scroll: it names the exact habit (tap Share) and the exact pain (things saved everywhere). It matches the store subtitle "Save ideas from any app" so search and gallery say the same thing.

## Supporting benefits (each maps to a real screen)
1. Everything on one wall, with its source -> screen: Wall (masonry cards with platform labels)
2. Find it again, even inside videos and articles -> screen: Search results
3. Organized without effort -> screen: Folder drawer / folder wall
4. Works with no signal -> screen: Wall or new note while offline (no spinner)
5. The full context, not a bare link -> screen: Idea detail (title, author, text, source)
6. Your ideas, in your AI -> screen: Account / Connect AI (Pro)

## Tone
Is: calm, clever, friendly. Never: loud, techy, salesy. Banned words: seamless, powerful, intuitive, all-in-one, AI-powered, revolutionary, second brain.
